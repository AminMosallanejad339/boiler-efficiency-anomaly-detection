## 3. Domain Knowledge (17 Years Industrial Experience)

### Steam Cycle
- Feedwater -> Boiler -> Main Steam -> Turbine -> Condenser -> Return
- Key parameters: temperature, pressure, steam flow

### Combustion
- Air-to-Fuel Ratio determines combustion efficiency
- Excess O2 causes heat loss
- Low O2 causes incomplete combustion and CO production

### Boiler Efficiency
- **Direct Method:** Efficiency = (Energy Output / Energy Input) x 100
- **Indirect Method (Heat Loss):** Efficiency = 100 - Total Losses
- Main losses: dry flue gas, water evaporation, fuel moisture, incomplete combustion, radiation

### Air Preheater (APH)
- Leakage reduces efficiency
- Effectiveness indicates heat exchange quality

### Emissions
- SO2: from sulfur in coal
- NOx: from nitrogen in air at high temperature
- CO: from incomplete combustion
- Dust: particulate matter
