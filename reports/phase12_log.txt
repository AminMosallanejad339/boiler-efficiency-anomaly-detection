[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  L1 Regularization:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: R(W) = λ * sum(|W|)
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Sparse weights, feature selection
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Automatic feature selection
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: Non-differentiable at 0
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  L2 Regularization:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: R(W) = λ * sum(W^2)
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Small weights, smooth
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Differentiable, stable
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: No feature selection
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Elastic Net:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: R(W) = λ1*sum(|W|) + λ2*sum(W^2)
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Combines L1 and L2
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Best of both
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: More hyperparameters
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Dropout:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: Randomly zero activations during training
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Prevents co-adaptation
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Effective, simple
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: Slower training
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Early Stopping:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: Stop when val_loss stops improving
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Prevents overfitting
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Simple, effective
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: Needs validation set
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Weight Decay:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: θ = θ - lr*(gradient + λ*θ)
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Shrinks weights
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Simple
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: Similar to L2
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Label Smoothing:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: y_smooth = (1-ε)*y + ε/K
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: Soft labels
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Better calibration
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: For classification only
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  Data Augmentation:
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Formula: Generate synthetic samples
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Effect: More training data
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Pros: Better generalization
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]     Cons: Not for tabular easily
[2026-09-16 13:21:21] 
  [SELECTED] L2 + Dropout + EarlyStopping
[2026-09-16 13:21:21]   Rationale: Proven combination for tabular MLPs
