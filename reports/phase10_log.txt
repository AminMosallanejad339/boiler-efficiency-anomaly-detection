[2026-09-16 13:13:07] STAGE 10.3: OBJECTIVE FUNCTION FORMULATION
[2026-09-16 13:13:07] ======================================================================
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   Total Objective: J_total = J_data + λ * J_reg
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   Data Loss: Binary Crossentropy or Categorical Crossentropy
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   Regularization: L2 (weight decay) + Dropout
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   λ (Lambda): 1e-4 (L2 regularization strength)
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   Formula:
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]     Anomaly: J = BCE(y, y_pred) + λ * sum(||W||^2)
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]     Binned: J = CE(y, y_pred) + λ * sum(||W||^2)
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]   Rationale:
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]     - Data loss ensures model fits training data
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]     - Regularization prevents overfitting
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]     - λ controls trade-off between fit and complexity
[2026-09-16 13:13:07]
